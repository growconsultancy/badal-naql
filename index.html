What employees should know

Who gets it, who pays

Employees and workers covered by the Labour Law, including public institutions that follow it. Your employer pays it.

How much

The value of 5 litres of gasoline per day. Never less than 500,000 LBP and never more than 800,000 LBP a day. Before 23 October 2026 it was a fixed 450,000 LBP a day.

Which days count

Only the days you actually go to your workplace. Days you don't attend are not counted.

Why it changes

The amount follows the official fuel price, which the Ministry of Energy updates about twice a week. This page updates on its own.

Source: Decree No. 4021, Official Gazette issue 46, 22 October 2026.
